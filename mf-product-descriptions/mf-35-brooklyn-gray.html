MaxFoot MF-35 Folding Electric Tricycle

The MaxFoot MF-35 is a folding electric tricycle for adults: a 750W rear-wheel-drive brushless motor (up to 1,200W peak), a removable 48V 13Ah (624Wh) lithium battery with up to 50 miles of range, Shimano 7-speed shifting, and a 230 lb rider + 80 lb cargo payload. It folds tool-free in under 10 seconds to 47 x 27.5 x 35.3 inches, has a 12-inch step-through standover height, and fits riders from 5'1" to 5'9".

Frequently Asked Questions

What you need to know about the MF-35 folding electric trike.

What are the folded dimensions of the MF-35?

When folded, the MF-35 folding electric tricycle measures 47" L x 27.5" W x 35.3" H. Its folding design helps reduce its storage footprint for garages, sheds, RV travel, and transport. Please measure your intended storage space before purchase.

Why choose the MF-35 folding electric trike?

The MF-35 folding electric trike gives you the confidence, carrying capability, and electric assist of a full-size trike without demanding full-time storage space. Use it for errands, neighborhood rides, and everyday cargo, then fold it down to make storage, RV travel, and transport more manageable.

Will the MF-35 folding electric tricycle fit in my car trunk?

The MF-35 folding electric tricycle folds for easier transport, but fit depends on your vehicle. It may fit in larger SUVs, vans, pickup beds, or vehicles with fold-flat rear seats. Please compare your vehicle's cargo space and payload capacity with the MF-35's folded dimensions and weight before purchase; two people may be needed for loading.

Who is the MF-35 folding electric tricycle best suited for?

The MF-35 folding electric tricycle is designed for adult riders who value the added confidence of three wheels, electric assistance for everyday trips, and practical capacity for errands or gear. Its adjustable saddle post and telescopic handlebar stem are designed to accommodate riders from 5'1" to 5'9".

What range can I expect on a charge?

The MF-35 folding electric trike offers a range of up to 50 miles under ideal riding conditions. Actual range varies based on rider and cargo weight, terrain, tire pressure, temperature, battery condition, assist level, throttle use, and riding style.

How long does the battery take to charge?

A full charge takes approximately 6.75 hours with the included charger. The 48V 13Ah battery is removable and rear-mounted, so you can take it off the trike and charge it indoors on a standard wall outlet.

How much weight can the MF-35 carry?

The MF-35 has a 230 lb rated rider capacity plus 80 lb of cargo capacity in the 30L rear basket, for a combined payload of 310 lbs. The chassis is steel-gusseted for strength.

What size rider does the MF-35 fit?

The MF-35 fits riders from 5'1" to 5'9". The low step-through frame has a 12-inch standover height, the saddle post adjusts from 27.5" to 38.5", and the handlebar height adjusts from 46.5" to 52.5".

Who Is the MF-35 Best For?

The MaxFoot MF-35 folding electric trike is built for adult riders who want:

  • Stable three-wheel confidence — three wheels, no balancing at a standstill, and a 12-inch step-through frame that is easier to mount and dismount than a standard top-tube bike.
  • Everyday electric assistance — 5 pedal-assist levels plus a half-twist throttle for errands, inclines, and longer routes.
  • Real carrying capacity — a 30L rear basket rated for 80 lbs of groceries, gear, or supplies.
  • Storage and transport freedom — folds tool-free in under 10 seconds to fit a garage, apartment, RV, or the back of a larger vehicle.
  • Riders from 5'1" to 5'9" — adjustable seat post and quick-tilt handlebar stem.

Typical use cases: grocery and errand runs, neighborhood and park riding, longer recreational outings, RV and small-space storage, and mobility support for riders who want electric help without a full-size, non-folding trike.
